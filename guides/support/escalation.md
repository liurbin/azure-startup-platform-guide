## Scope and prerequisites

Mode: reference or authorized read-only. No secrets, prompt contents, invoices or raw customer logs in Git. Support submissions, applications and evidence sharing require approval.
Capture a sanitized warning/error, source, scope, UTC interval, business impact and any deadline. Use official portal surfaces to validate emailed requests.

## Official policy and live state

Billing, startup benefits, quota applications, limited-access reviews and technical incidents have different routes. Technical support entitlement is not inferred from credits, cloud spend or a resource role. Service Health events and resource health are related but distinct views.

## Read-only checks

1. Classify the issue: benefit/binding, billing/invoice, quota/capacity, access/policy, service incident, model lifecycle, or safety/abuse review.
2. Open the matching official program form, account billing help, service quota surface, limited-access guidance or Azure Help + support. Inspect support-plan/agreement eligibility and the user's ability to open a request.
3. Check applicable Service Health notices and existing requests before opening duplicates. Record current status, assigned owner, requested information and relevant deadlines.
4. Prepare only scope aliases, service/deployment type, sanitized error code/message, UTC interval, operation/request/correlation IDs, and relevant quota/configuration observations. Exclude payloads, credentials and unnecessary personal data.

## Interpretation and decisions

State verified facts, hypotheses and missing evidence separately. Do not choose incident severity solely to accelerate a request; use the official impact criteria and actual business impact. Acknowledgment or "submitted" is not approval or resolution.

## Changes and approval

Have the authorized owner review application attestations, severity, recipients and evidence before submission. Follow compliance/abuse notices through their designated review process; do not disable controls or invent another identity to avoid a restriction.

## Verification and escalation

Track request status and the next responsible party. After approval/fix, revisit the original resource and recheck the requested state in the same scope. If quota, policy or billing records still disagree, reopen with before/after evidence. Redact again before any public issue or external-agent sharing.
